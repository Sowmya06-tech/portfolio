01

Marxcel

GTM · POSITIONING · ACQUISITION · SALES · MARKETING OPS · AI

Building the growth engine behind a B2B marketing business.

The challenge

Marxcel needed more than marketing execution. The role spanned positioning, GTM, acquisition, sales, marketing operations, client growth and international expansion.

My role Marketing, Client Strategy & Sales — an embedded, consulting-style growth role across marketing, sales and client strategy, not a conventional marketing-manager seat.

Read the case study +

The approach

Treat growth as one connected system rather than separate marketing and sales tasks: Position → Target → Acquire → Convert → Grow. Positioning and ICP decide who to go after; research and data drive acquisition; sales process and pipeline handle conversion; account strategy grows existing clients.

The work

  • Marketing & GTM strategy, positioning and messaging
  • ICP, target-market and target-company research
  • Prospect and company databases, with lead enrichment
  • Outbound systems and AI-assisted prospecting
  • Lead-generation and sales strategy, pipeline development and sales trackers
  • Marketing operations, automation and the marketing-to-sales process
  • Existing-client growth and account strategy
  • International market expansion and prospecting, including the US and Singapore
  • Revenue and growth strategy

The proof

  • ICP & target-company research
  • Prospect / company databases
  • Outbound systems
  • Sales trackers
  • Marketing automation
  • AI-assisted prospecting & lead enrichment
  • GTM plans
  • International prospecting: US & Singapore

What I built

A connected growth engine — positioning and ICP, target databases, outbound and AI-assisted prospecting, sales tracking and account strategy — designed so acquisition, conversion and client growth run as one process instead of separate efforts.
